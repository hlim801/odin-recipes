Brown Butter Chocolate Chip Recipe

Description

This recipe creates around 12 servings of cookies.

Ingredients

  • 150g butter
  • 200g light brown sugar Melt most of the way, then cool to keep the texture right. If using unsalted, taste and add salt.
  • 50g white sugar
  • 2 large egg: One will be used just for the yolk.
  • 220g flour
  • 3/4 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract

Steps

  1. Melt butter on low heat Will bubble till it turns golden brown. COOL DOWN
  2. Mix Brown sugar and White sugar
  3. Mix 1 large egg, 1 egg yolk, vanilla
  4. Mix flour, baking soda, salt salt is to strengthen the sweet flavor, be careful
  5. Mix in chocolate chips
  6. Roll into small balls roughly 2oz balls. add extra chips for visuals
  7. Chill the cookie cover with thin foil and put them in the fridge for atleast 30 mins.
  8. Bake at 350F(175C) for 11~12 minutes sprinkly some sea salt above to enhance sweetness. careful
  9. Enjoy!
