Edible Cookie Dough Recipe

Description

This recipe creates 4 servings of Edible cookie dough. It is a extremely easy recipe, take less than 30mins to make.

I can honestly say, as an edible cookie dough expert, this is the best you’ll ever try!

Ingredients

  • 94g Flour: Heat-treat to kill bacteria. Toasting adds a nutty flavor.
  • 71g UnSalted butter: Melt most of the way, then cool to keep the texture right. If using unsalted, taste and add salt.
  • 56g Brown & 38g white sugar:
  • 5g Vanilla Extract: Adds that classic cookie dough flavor.
  • 15g milk
  • 48g Mini chocolate chips:

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F(175C) Spread flour on a baking sheet and toast for 5~7 minutes. Let cool.
  2. Melt butter most of the way, then cool to room temp. Mix with brown and white sugar until smooth.
  3. Stir in vanilla and milk, then add cooled flour and chocolate chips. Adjust milk for desired texture.
  4. If dough is too soft, chill for 10 minutes.
  5. Enjoy!
